What is Southern Spear?

Tactical, readable and team-focused

Southern Spear rewards communication, preparation and role discipline. Players see their own team as the Commonwealth Land Service, and the other team as the fictional Murasian Armed Forces. Presentation changes appearance only: gameplay tuning is mechanically equal for both teams.

01 · Train

Learn before deployment

Core movement, weapon and role training before taking responsibility in a match.

Qualifications unlock roles, not power.

02 · Operate

Play a defined role

Rifleman, grenadier, support gunner, medic and command roles contribute differently.

Team capability matters more than individual score.

03 · Overcome

Complete the objective

Coordinate movement, control terrain and complete the mission with the team intact.

No lone-wolf hero loop.

These are design goals. Training, qualifications and roles are not yet implemented; see the roadmap for what exists today.
