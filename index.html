The rundown

What is Southern Spear?

Southern Spear is a first-person tactical shooter in the tradition of the classic team-based military shooters: realistic pacing, small teams, clear roles, and objectives that only fall to coordinated play.

It is set in a fictional world inspired by Australia. You fight across outback stations, dry creek lines and red-earth country as a soldier of the fictional 3rd Battalion, Australian Commonwealth Regiment (3 ACR). Matches are Objective Assault: two teams fight over a chain of objectives that fall in order, round after round.

There are no loot boxes, no pay-to-win and no hero classes. Every weapon and every character is mechanically identical on both sides.

  • 01

    Grounded

    First-person only, measured movement, weapons that reward fundamentals.

  • 02

    Team first

    Objectives are taken by holding the ring. Opposing soldiers inside contest it, so the fight comes to you.

  • 03

    Fair by design

    Both teams are identical. Only how they look changes, and only to the viewer.

One battle, two perspectives

Everyone is 3 ACR. Everyone else is the enemy.

Both teams play as the same side. On your screen, your team wears 3 ACR's CMECU uniform and carries the A88. The other team appears as the Murasian Armed Forces (MAF): a fictional conventional opposing force in red-earth colours. On their screens it is exactly the reverse. Nobody is ever stuck playing the bad guys.
